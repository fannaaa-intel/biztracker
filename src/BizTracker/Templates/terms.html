3. Data privacy (Republic Act No. 10173 – Data Privacy Act of 2012)

  • The system processes personal information: names, addresses, contact details, business and tax records, employee lists and health certificate records. Health information is sensitive personal information.
  • This information is collected and used only to process permits, taxes, inspections and certificates, and to meet the legal duties of the LGU (RA 7160, RA 11032). It is not sold or shared with others except as required by law.
  • Staff must keep this information confidential, use it only for their official work, and must not copy, print or disclose it without a lawful reason.
  • Data subjects have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Requests go to the LGU's Data Protection Officer.
  • By accepting, you consent to the processing of your personal information for these purposes.

4. Accuracy of information and documents

  • Everything you enter or upload must be true, complete and genuine.
  • False statements or falsified documents may lead to the denial or revocation of a permit and to liability under the applicable laws.

5. Records and audit log

  • Logins, logouts, changes, uploads and printed documents are recorded in an audit log with your name and the time.
  • Records are never deleted: mistakes are corrected or voided with a reason, so the history stays complete.
