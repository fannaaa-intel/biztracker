Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) · Mayor's Permit to operate a business

Legal basis

RA 7160 – Local Government Code RA 11032 – Ease of Doing Business Act Municipal Revenue Code
  • Every business operating in the municipality needs a Mayor's Permit and pays the local business tax (RA 7160, Sec. 143 and 444).
  • Applications are filed once at the Business One-Stop Shop and processed within the RA 11032 time limits: renewal (simple) {{arta_simple_days}} working days, new business (complex) {{arta_complex_days}} working days.

Process

  1. Submitted → Under Review (documents checked) → For Assessment
  2. Assessed: the business tax is computed from the gross receipts (renewal) or capital investment (new).
  3. Paid: the tax is paid at the Treasurer's Office (partial payments are accepted).
  4. Issued: the Mayor's Permit is released only when the tax and any charges are fully paid.
