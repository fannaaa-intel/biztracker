Office of the Municipal Assessor · Office of the Municipal Treasurer

Legal basis

RA 7160 – Local Government Code, Book II, Title II
  • Real property (land, buildings and machinery) is assessed by the Assessor and taxed every year on its assessed value.
  • A Special Education Fund (SEF) tax is collected on top of the basic tax (RA 7160, Sec. 235).
  • The RPT clearance is one of the clearances endorsed to the BPLO before the Mayor's Permit is issued.

QuarterDue on or before
1st quarterMarch 31

Quarters are paid in order. Partial payments are accepted at the Treasurer's Office; each walk-in payment gets an Official Receipt (AF 51). A quarter is Paid only when its whole amount is paid.

Penalty for late payment (RA 7160, Sec. 255)

Interest{{rpt_penalty_rate_monthly|pct}} per month on the unpaid amount of a past-due quarter (a part of a month counts as a month).

Order of paymentEach payment pays the penalty first, then the tax.

Tax clearance

  • Issued when every quarter already due is fully paid.
  • Requirements: the latest Official Receipts, or the tax declaration (TD No.) / property index number (PIN).

Unpaid real property tax becomes a lien on the property and may lead to its levy and sale at public auction.
