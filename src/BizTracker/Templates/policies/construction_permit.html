Office of the Building Official (OBO) · Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)

Legal basis

PD 1096 – National Building Code RA 9514 – Fire Code of the Philippines Zoning Ordinance / CLUP RA 11032 – Ease of Doing Business Act
  • No building may be built, renovated, extended or demolished without a Building Permit from the Building Official (PD 1096).
  • A Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) from the BFP is needed before the Building Permit (RA 9514).
  • The project must conform to the zoning of its location (Locational / Zoning Clearance from the MPDO).
  • Processing time (highly technical transaction, RA 11032): {{arta_technical_days}} working days.

Documentary requirements

  • Architectural and structural plans, signed and sealed
  • Electrical plans
  • Plumbing / sanitary plans
  • Lot title and tax declaration

Schedule

  • The applicant gives the estimated start and estimated completion of construction.
  • A project still going after its estimated completion is shown as Behind Schedule and an alert is raised.
  • The building may be used only after the Certificate of Occupancy is approved.

Building without a permit, or using a building without a Certificate of Occupancy, may lead to a work stoppage order and penalties.
