Joint Inspection Team (Building Official, Municipal Engineering, Bureau of Fire Protection)

Legal basis

PD 1096 – National Building Code RA 9514 – Fire Code of the Philippines RA 11032 – Ease of Doing Business Act
  • Business establishments are inspected every year to make sure the building stays safe to occupy (PD 1096) and meets fire safety rules (RA 9514).
  • Inspections are done jointly in one visit, after the permit is issued, so the business is not visited by each office separately (RA 11032).
  • Processing time (complex transaction): {{arta_complex_days}} working days.

What is inspected

StructuralStructural soundness, no unauthorized changes to the building.
ElectricalWiring, panel boards and loads.
MechanicalAir-conditioning, generators, elevators and other equipment.
FireFire extinguishers, exits, signage and alarms (BFP).

Process and result

  1. Scheduled: the visit date is set and the business is notified.
  2. In Progress: each department records Passed or Failed with its findings.
  3. All four Passed: the inspection is Completed and an Annual Inspection Certificate (AIC) is issued.
  4. Any Failed: the business corrects the findings and a re-inspection is scheduled.

Reminders

  • BizTracker reminds the business {{expiry_warning_days}} days before a scheduled visit or re-inspection.
  • The certificate covers one inspection year.

Serious violations, especially fire hazards, can lead to suspension of operations until corrected.
