Municipal Health Office (MHO) · Health certificates of employees

Legal basis

PD 856 – Code on Sanitation of the Philippines RA 7160 – Local Government Code
  • Every employee, especially a food handler, must have a valid health certificate issued by the local health office after a medical examination (PD 856 and its implementing rules).
  • Valid health certificates of all employees are a requirement of the Sanitary Permit.

Who needs one

Food HandlerEmployees who prepare, cook, serve or pack food or drinks (cook, baker, server, packer).
Non-FoodAll other employees (cashier, guard, driver, utility staff).
Separated employeesEmployees who no longer work at the business are marked Separated. They are not counted and need no certificate; their certificate history is kept on record.

Requirements

  • Medical examination and the laboratory tests required by the health office (e.g. chest X-ray, stool and urine examination for food handlers)
  • Health education seminar, when required by the health office
  • Valid ID
