Municipal Health Office (MHO) · Sanitary Permit to operate

Legal basis

PD 856 – Code on Sanitation of the Philippines RA 7160 – Local Government Code RA 11032 – Ease of Doing Business Act
  • No establishment, especially one that prepares, sells or serves food, may operate without a Sanitary Permit from the local health office (PD 856 and its implementing rules).
  • The Sanitary Permit is one of the clearances endorsed to the BPLO before the Mayor's Permit is issued.
  • Processing time (complex transaction, RA 11032): {{arta_complex_days}} working days.

Categories

FoodRestaurants, eateries, bakeries, food stalls and other businesses that handle food or drinks.
Non-FoodEvery other establishment (retail, services, offices).

Documentary requirements

  • Microbiological water test (potable water)
  • Physico-chemical water analysis
  • Pest control certificate
  • Valid health certificates of all employees (see Health Certificates)

Process

  1. Submitted (intake of the application and documents)
  2. Lab Analysis (water test results verified)
  3. For Inspection: on-site sanitary inspection. The establishment must score at least {{sp_passing_score}} / 100.
  4. Issued: the Sanitary Permit is released. A failed inspection is corrected and inspected again.

Validity

ValidityUntil December 31 of the year it was issued; renew every year.
Expiry warningBizTracker warns {{expiry_warning_days}} days before the permit expires.
DisplayThe permit must be posted in a conspicuous place in the establishment.

The health office may suspend or revoke the permit when sanitary rules are violated.
